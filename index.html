About Me

I'm Rushikesh Nerkar, an 18-year-old security researcher and dedicated lab grinder. My work lives in the offensive security space, where I spend my time deep in complex environments figuring out exactly how systems break and documenting the full methodology.

This portfolio represents my journey through offensive security research. I don't just post solutions — I break down the methodology. You'll find a heavy emphasis on modern infrastructure, internal networks, and web-scale vulnerabilities.
